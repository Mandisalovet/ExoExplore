About the Project

ExoExplore is an interactive educational project centred on exoplanets and space science. It provides a fun way for users to engage with concepts related to planets outside our solar system.

This is a collaborative project. I contributed to its development and learning experience.

Project Focus

Making astronomy more approachable through interactive learning, educational content and an engaging quiz format.

What Interested Me

Exoplanet research connects astronomy, physics and the search for worlds beyond our own. Projects like this offer an opportunity to make scientific ideas accessible through technology.
