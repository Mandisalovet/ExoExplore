
# ExoExplore 🌌

An interactive educational quiz game exploring exoplanets and the worlds beyond our Solar System.

## 🚀 Explore the Project

- **[Play ExoExplore](https://siphokhoza.github.io/ExoExplore/)**
- **[View the Project Landing Page](https://mandisalovet.github.io/ExoExplore/)**

## About the Project

ExoExplore is a collaborative educational project designed to make learning about exoplanets more engaging and accessible through an interactive quiz experience.

The project introduces users to the science of planets beyond our Solar System, encouraging curiosity about astronomy and planetary systems.

## Project Focus

- Exploring exoplanets and planetary science.
- Making astronomy more accessible through interactive learning.
- Combining scientific content with an engaging quiz format.

## My Contribution

I contributed to the collaborative development of ExoExplore. The project gave me an opportunity to engage with the use of technology in science communication and educational experiences.

## Why Exoplanets?

Exoplanets are planets that orbit stars beyond our Sun. Their discovery and study help us understand planetary systems, how planets form and evolve, and the possibility of worlds with conditions different from those in our Solar System.
